NMLify

Convert a Spotify playlist into a Traktor crate (.nml), entirely in your browser.

1. Spotify access

NMLify ships with a built-in Spotify app, so most users can skip straight to Log in with Spotify below.

Spotify keeps third-party apps in a restricted developer mode unless the developer is an organization with thousands of monthly users. If the login fails for you, register a free Spotify app of your own and paste its Client ID here.
